What is the Predictive Priority Index?

PPI is a 0 to 100 score that ranks infrastructure sites for further assessment. It combines four inputs, each scaled to 0 to 1:

  • Historical failure frequency: damage records near the site, plus its distance to the nearest recurring damage cluster found by DBSCAN.
  • Structural vulnerability: condition score from 1 (very low) to 5 (critical).
  • Flood exposure: number of past flood events (of 78 in NC-FLEX) that reached the site, informed by estimated flood depth.
  • Community observations: reviewed 311 report count. A supporting signal only.

Weights. Each slider value is divided by the sum of all four, so the weights always total 1.0. PPI = 100 x (w1 x failures + w2 x vulnerability + w3 x exposure + w4 x reports). Raising one slider lowers the share of the others.

70 and aboveSchedule a field inspection and engineering review.
40 to 69Add to the monitoring list and verify the underlying records.
Below 40Routine schedule. This does not mean the site is safe, especially where reports are sparse.

The score ranks sites for attention. It cannot replace an engineering assessment, and final decisions stay with qualified professionals.
